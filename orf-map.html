Where the versions disagree

lacI: 202 or 370?

202 → 370

The ORF at 12–329 (frame 2) covers the ATG at 202 that starts the lacI ORF (frame 0). Without a lookahead the search resumes at 330, so both online versions start lacI at the next ATG in its frame, 370.

lacY: 4410 or 4656?

4410 → 4656

With +, short ORFs count during the search. A 46-codon ORF at 4376–4516 in another frame uses up lacY's real ATG at 4410. The later filter removes that short ORF, but the damage is done. {100,} avoids this one only because that ORF is too short to match.

What no ATG regex can fix

GTG 79 · TTG 5727

lacI starts at GTG and lacA at TTG, so every version reports them short, by 123 and 6 bp. Allowing alternative starts and checking the RBS (lab step 7) is the next step.

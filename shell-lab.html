The files in ~/data

Small on purpose, so you can check every answer by eye. The formats are the real ones.

FileFormatWhat is in it
sequences.fastaFASTA6 nucleotide sequences (human, mouse, SARS-CoV-2), wrapped at 60 bases per line
primers.fastaFASTA3 PCR primers for 16S rRNA and fungal ITS
reads.fastqFASTQ10 Illumina reads, 4 lines each; 3 end in adapter sequence AGATCGGAAGAGC
SRR1039509.fastq.gzFASTQ, gzip3 more reads, compressed. Read it with zcat
genes.gffGFF322 gene, exon and CDS features on chr1, chr2, chr17 and chrX
variants.vcfVCF 4.213 variant calls with QUAL scores and PASS / LowQual filters
samples.tsvTSVSample sheet for 6 RNA-seq libraries: condition, tissue, replicate, read count
triplets.txtTextCodon triplets from this course's triplets.txt
Everything runs in your browser. Files you create are kept on this device between visits; Reset files restores the originals. The practice shell covers the everyday subset of bash and GNU tools, so a few rarely used options are missing.

Exercise grader

Drop the students' BIO116-genome-browser-*.json files here. The grader rebuilds each student's questions from the question-set code in their file, scores the auto-graded parts, and flags files that were edited after saving, where the student tried to paste, or where two students submitted the same question set. Files are read in this browser only and are not uploaded anywhere. Don't share this page with students: it shows the answer keys.

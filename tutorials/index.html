Sequence Analysis Tutorials

Six interactive tutorials that take you from aligning two proteins to building a family tree. Every tutorial uses the same five globins, so each new method can be tested on sequences you already know. Everything runs in your browser; there is nothing to install.

  • 6 tutorials, about 5 hours in total
  • Take them in order
  • Assumes: amino-acid chemistry, basic probability

Tutorials

  1. 1

    Aligning the Globins

    Pairwise alignmentAbout 45 minutes

    How to score and build an alignment of two proteins. Fill a Needleman–Wunsch or Smith–Waterman matrix one cell at a time, align whole globins with affine gaps, and test whether a score could be chance.

    Tools: BLOSUM62 explorer · step-through DP matrix · globin aligner with dot plot · shuffle test
  2. 2

    Inside BLAST

    Database searchingAbout 50 minutes

    How BLAST skips almost the whole alignment matrix: neighborhood words, two-hit seeding, X-drop extension and E-values. Then run a mini-BLAST and PSI-BLAST, and learn to run real BLAST and read its output.

    Tools: neighborhood explorer · hit map · X-drop chart · E-value calculator · mini-BLAST · PSI-BLAST iterations
  3. 3

    PAM and BLOSUM

    Scoring matricesAbout 45 minutes

    Where substitution scores come from. Build a BLOSUM-style matrix from a real globin block, extrapolate a PAM series, and see why each matrix suits one evolutionary distance.

    Tools: block-to-matrix builder · DNA PAM extrapolator · eight-matrix explorer · one pair, eight matrices
  4. 4

    Profile HMMs and HMMER

    Family modelsAbout 55 minutes

    Turn a family alignment into a profile hidden Markov model. Score sequences with Viterbi and Forward, calibrate E-values, and learn the HMMER programs and their output.

    Tools: live model diagram · sequence logo · mini-hmmsearch with training choices · Stockholm export
  5. 5

    Aligning Many Globins

    Multiple sequence alignmentAbout 50 minutes

    Align all five globins at once with the progressive method behind Clustal. Build the guide tree, watch gaps freeze at each merge, test all 120 merge orders, and learn to run and judge modern aligners.

    Tools: distance matrix and guide tree · progressive aligner · all-orders experiment · Clustal and FASTA export
  6. 6

    Globin Family Trees

    Molecular phylogenyAbout 55 minutes

    Turn the globin alignment into a tree. Correct distances for multiple substitutions, build a neighbor-joining tree step by step, score all 15 trees by parsimony, test support with the bootstrap, and meet likelihood and Bayesian methods.

    Tools: distance corrections and saturation chart · neighbor-joining stepper · parsimony over all trees · bootstrap · alignment export for IQ-TREE

The five globins

All five fold into the same eight-helix shape and hold heme between a distal (E7) and a proximal (F8) histidine. Whether an alignment puts those histidines in the same column is the check used throughout the course.

Follow leghemoglobin through the course

Soybean leghemoglobin is homologous to the animal globins but sits in the twilight zone of sequence identity. Each tutorial tests a new method on it.

  1. 1 · Pairwise alignmentAligned to β-globin with BLOSUM62, both heme histidines line up. With identity scoring they do not. Against shuffled sequences, the real score stands well clear.
  2. 2 · BLAST and PSI-BLASTBLAST misses it at every threshold. PSI-BLAST picks up the myoglobins but still cannot reach leghemoglobin, because the small database has no intermediate sequences.
  3. 3 · Scoring matricesMatrices built for close relatives shrink its alignment with β-globin to a fragment: 4 residues with PAM30, 48 with PAM70. Matrices for distant relatives keep an alignment of about 90 residues.
  4. 4 · Profile HMMsA model trained on hemoglobins finds it in the course database, but not at Swiss-Prot scale. Adding near-identical myoglobins to the training set tilts the model away from it.
  5. 5 · Multiple alignmentThe guide tree adds it last. With cheap gap extension, its distal histidine slides 18 columns out of place.
  6. 6 · PhylogenyAs the outgroup it roots the tree, but its long branch tugs on β-globin: in about a third of bootstrap replicates the two pair up, a small case of long-branch attraction.

Notes

  • Each tutorial opens in a new tab. The links work only for people the pages have been shared with.
  • The in-browser tools are teaching versions of the real algorithms. Each tutorial says what it simplifies and shows how to run the real software (BLAST+, HMMER, Clustal Omega, MAFFT, MUSCLE).
  • Sequences are the UniProt entries above, with the initiator methionine removed so residue numbers match the classic literature.

Background in two minutes

Anfinsen showed that for many small proteins the sequence alone determines the fold, so structure should be computable. Levinthal pointed out that a 100-residue chain has about 3100 ≈ 1048 conformations, so neither the protein nor a computer can search them all. Prediction methods are shortcuts built from what we already know about proteins:

1970sChou–Fasman and GOR: residue propensities for helix and strand. Q3 ≈ 50–65%.
1990sHomology modelling and threading: copy the shape of a related protein with a known structure.
2000sRosetta fragment assembly, PSIPRED profiles (Q3 ≈ 80%).
2010sCo-evolution: residues that mutate together are usually in contact.
2020AlphaFold2 reaches near-experimental accuracy in CASP14.
2022–24ESMFold (language model, no MSA); AlphaFold3 and Boltz add ligands, DNA and RNA.

Why do exons 1–3 sit in frame 1 but exon 4 in frame 2?

Introns 1 and 2 are 267 and 153 nt long, both multiples of 3, so the frame doesn't change, even though codon 97 (Arg, AGG) is split AG|G across intron 2. Intron 3 is 697 nt long, which is not a multiple of 3, so exon 4 moves to frame 2. Translating each frame separately can't join these pieces; splicing can.

Step 3 of 7

What the sequence alone tells you

Before building a 3D model, simple statistics already hint at the structure: whether the protein is soluble or sits in a membrane, how much of it is helix or strand, and whether it looks folded at all.

Load

Hydropathy plot (Kyte–Doolittle)

Each point is the average hydrophobicity of a window centred on that residue. A 19-residue window above 1.6 suggests a helix that spans the membrane.

Secondary structure: Chou–Fasman

Average each residue's helix propensity (Pα) and strand propensity (Pβ) over a window. Call helix if ⟨Pα⟩ passes its threshold and beats ⟨Pβ⟩, strand if ⟨Pβ⟩ passes its threshold, otherwise coil. Then drop helices shorter than 4 and strands shorter than 3. For H-Ras, the prediction is scored against the crystal structure 5P21.

Discussion

Tuning thresholds on one protein is overfitting: the settings won't carry over to the next protein. Chou–Fasman ignores two things modern predictors rely on: evolutionary information (the same position in related sequences) and long-range contacts. β-strands pair with partners that can be far apart in the sequence, and a local window can't see that.

The longest frame 2 ORF is a useful negative control. It is about 18% proline and made of near-perfect 28-residue repeats, which strongly suggests it doesn't fold.

What the network does

MSA and co-evolution. If residue i mutates and residue j compensates across many related species, the two are probably in contact. AlphaFold2's Evoformer reasons over the alignment and a residue-pair map through 48 blocks.

Structure module. Each residue is treated as a rigid frame that is rotated and moved into place. The model is recycled three times and outputs coordinates together with pLDDT and PAE. AlphaFold3 replaces this step with diffusion, which also lets it place ligands.

Step 5 of 7

Judge the model

A prediction is a hypothesis. Predictors store per-residue confidence (pLDDT, 0–100) in the B-factor column of the PDB file. Load your model below; the file is read in your browser and never uploaded.

Compare with experiment

Download PDB 5P21 ↗ (H-Ras 1–166 with GppNHp and Mg²⁺) and load it here. The Cα RMSD is computed after optimal superposition, over the residue range above, using residues numbered the same in both files.

Rules of thumb: Cα RMSD under 2 Å is very close. For TM-score (above 0.5 means the same fold), use the RCSB pairwise alignment ↗.

What to expect for H-Ras. The G domain (1–166) should be almost entirely above 90. The hypervariable region (about 167–189, ending in the lipid-anchored CVLS) should drop below 50. It is flexible in the cell and not visible in the crystal either. Compare the RMSD over 1–166 with the RMSD over 1–189.

PAE (predicted aligned error, the heat map in ColabFold and AlphaFold DB) tells you how confident the model is about the relative position of two residues. High pLDDT with high PAE between two domains means each domain is right, but their relative orientation is not known. For complexes, ipTM above about 0.8 means a confident interface.

Step 6 of 7

What predictors can't tell you

H-Ras makes a good case study because its biology depends on details that a single static model misses.

One conformation, two states

Ras switches between inactive GDP-bound and active GTP-bound states. The changes are concentrated in switch I (≈30–38) and switch II (≈59–76). AlphaFold2 gives you only one of them.

Point mutations

Predict G12V: the model looks almost the same as wild type. The mutation is oncogenic because it blocks GTP hydrolysis, not because it changes the fold.

Ligands and lipids

AlphaFold2 has no GTP, no Mg²⁺ and no farnesyl lipid. AlphaFold3 can add GTP and Mg²⁺, but most modifications are still out of reach.

Partners, membranes, motion

Ras works at the membrane, bound to Raf, SOS and GAP. Low pLDDT can mean real, functional disorder. A predicted model doesn't show dynamics or the folding pathway.
